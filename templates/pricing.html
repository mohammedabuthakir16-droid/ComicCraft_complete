Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use ComicCraft completely for free?

Yes! The Spark Creator plan is completely free forever. You can write stories, generate 4-panel visual comic strips, use the interactive soundboard, and download PDFs without entering a credit card.

How does Google Gemini power the comics?

ComicCraft uses Google Gemini to read your premise, invent dramatic pacing, write character dialogue balloons, structure onomatopoeia sound effects, and engineer rich visual inking prompts for each panel.

Can I use my own Gemini API Key?

Absolutely. Click the API Key button on the Dynamic Island dock to enter your own Google AI Studio key. This gives you direct access to your personal Gemini quota and latest experimental models.

Do I own the copyright to comics I create?

On Studio Pro and Omniverse plans, you own full commercial publishing rights to your generated artwork, characters, and storylines for webtoon monetization, print sales, and merchandise.
